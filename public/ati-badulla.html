Fostering technological innovation, practical software engineering, and technical leadership in Sri Lanka under SLIATE.

ATI Badulla is the leading tertiary technology institute in Uva Province, empowering students through high-quality higher national diploma programs, state-of-the-art computer labs, and active student developer communities.
